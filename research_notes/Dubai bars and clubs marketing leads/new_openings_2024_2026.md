# New Dubai bar, lounge and nightclub openings (Jan 2024 – Oct 2026) with weak online presence: sales-prospecting leads

**Researcher scope:** recent openings and relaunches city-wide, as of 17 Oct 2026. Other researchers cover each area in general, so every venue has an Area field to make merging easier.

**Method and limitations (read first):**
- **No pages could be opened directly.** WebFetch failed on DNS (`getaddrinfo ENOTFOUND`) for whatson.ae, timeoutdubai.com, factmagazines.com and whatshotinuae.com. curl through the agent proxy got `403 CONNECT tunnel failed` for every host tested, including whatson.ae, timeoutdubai.com, google.com, instagram.com and example.com. So I could not run the planned homepage checks (holding page, copyright year, viewport meta, SSL).
- **All contacts and web-presence evidence come from WebSearch summaries** of the cited URLs, not from reading the pages myself. Each source URL is the page the summary named. **Re-check every phone number and email on the live page before outreach.**
- **The search budget ran out after about 60 queries** (a 200-query limit shared by every agent in this turn). The list falls short of the 30-venue target: **17 rows are verified to some degree and 9 are provisional (marked "P").** A follow-up session could finish the provisional rows and look for more independent lounges (JVC, Al Barsha, Al Furjan, Dubai Hills, Festival City).
- **No Instagram follower counts or last-post dates were found.** Instagram could not be reached and search summaries did not include them.

## Which bars, lounges and nightclubs opened in Dubai in 2024, 2025 and 2026?

### Takeaway
Openings in 2024–26 cluster around hotel bars (JW Marriott Marquis, The Lana, W Mina Seyahi, Hilton Al Habtoor City, Sofitel Downtown), group-run hidden bars and sports lounges (Iris/Addmind, AlphaMind, ACT, Fundamental), and the new licensed street **25 Jump Street at One Central**. That street launched on 16–17 Jan 2026, with more Beirut-born bars arriving in Oct 2026. Truly independent, Instagram-led openings are fewer. GABA (Dubai Marina), LucyLu, Le Trottoir de Paloma (25 Jump Street) and The Black Horse (The Greens, pre-opening) are the clearest.

### Inferences
- Many "new bars" are hotel outlets or brand extensions from big groups (Addmind/Iris, FIVE, McGettigan's, Paris Society, Caprice). These usually sit under the hotel or group website. That counts as weak presence for a single venue, but the buyer is a group marketing team rather than an owner-operator.
- 25 Jump Street (One Central, Trade Centre) is the densest new cluster of independently branded, Instagram-led bars. It is the best area-specific prospecting target for late 2026.

### Gaps
- No monthly or quarterly roundups could be read in full, because fetching was blocked. Venue names came from search summaries, so smaller openings in JVC, Al Barsha, Al Furjan, Dubai Hills and Festival City are under-represented.
- Exact opening months could not be confirmed for Black TAPE Club, Music Loves Company, Harbour Bar, Ay-Vy and DOTS.
