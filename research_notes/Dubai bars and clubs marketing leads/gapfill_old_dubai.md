## Q1. Which bars and clubs exist in the not-yet-checked old-Dubai hotels in 2026, and which African or Arabic clubs operate in Deira and Bur Dubai?

### Takeaway
I found **27 new venues that count toward the target**, plus one possible duplicate (N26) and two updates to earlier rows.
- **Deira side, 16 venues.** These are in chain hotels (Sheraton, Hilton, Pullman, Novotel/ibis, InterContinental Festival City, Premier Inn, Crowne Plaza) and in independent hotels (Fortune Pearl, Lords, Asiana, Panorama Deira, Hues Boutique, Broadway, Lotus Grand).
- **Bur Dubai side, 11 countable venues.** These are in Mövenpick, Holiday Inn (now trading under other names), Citymax, Ramada, Dhow Palace, Arabian Park, Hilton Garden Inn Al Mina and Majestic Premier, plus two standalone clubs.
- **Arabic-format clubs:** Samarkhana Khaleeji Club (Hues Boutique Hotel, Deira), Moulin Rouge (Broadway Hotel, Deira), Omni Club (Wafi; an update to an earlier row), and Club Mojo's "Arabic Night" (Bur Dubai).
- **African clubs:** none beyond the two already in the earlier Deira notes (Lucy, African Beats West).
- **Status:** none of these venues could be confirmed open as of October 2026.
