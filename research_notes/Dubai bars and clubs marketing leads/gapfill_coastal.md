# Gap-fill pass: bars, lounges, pool/beach bars and nightclubs in Dubai coastal resort hotels (Palm Jumeirah, Bluewaters, Dubai Harbour, Umm Suqeim, Madinat Jumeirah / Jumeirah Beach Rd, Al Sufouh). New sales leads plus updates to earlier "names only" rows

Date context: research done 17 October 2026. This file adds to `palm_bluewaters_harbour_beach.md` and does not repeat any venue already in that file's lead table or excluded list.

**Limitations of this run (read first):**
- **Every finding comes from WebSearch result summaries.** No page fetches were tried, as instructed: WebFetch and curl were known to be blocked. So none of these could be checked: footer years, viewport/SSL, live Instagram follower counts, or what the hotel outlet pages actually show for contacts. Where a search summary named a fact without saying which result page it came from, that is flagged ("attribution unclear").
- **All 35 allotted searches were used.** Many venues below are known from a single listing. Status is "unverified 2026" unless a 2026-dated source is cited. Only Dhow & Anchor has 2026-dated evidence (reviews from Feb 2026).
- **No Instagram handle or follower count turned up for any venue in the main table.** The only handles seen belong to two excluded, well-marketed Club Vista Mare brands. Every "Instagram: not found" below means not found in search snippets. It does not mean the venue has no account.
- Phone numbers and emails are given only as published. Hotel switchboards and central reservation lines are labelled as such. No email or phone was guessed or built from a naming pattern. Numbers and emails on reseller or data-broker sites were thrown out.

## Which bars/lounges exist inside these coastal hotels in 2026, and which are under-marketed (hotel sub-page only, no own Instagram, stale or conflicting listings)?

### Takeaway
This pass found **29 new venues** across 15 coastal hotels or complexes, plus one update to an earlier row (FRANK Meat & Taps). Almost every new lead fits the pattern from the first pass: a hotel bar that exists only as a chain-site sub-page (Taj, Marriott/Aloft, Hyatt, Radisson, Jumeirah, One&Only), with no outlet phone, email or Instagram of its own. The weakest footprints are four:
- **Emerald Palace Kempinski's Sablanc beach bar and Skorpeus nightclub.** Only opening-era press can be found; a direct name search returned nothing relevant.
- **Koubba Bar at Al Qasr.** A direct name search returned nothing.
- **Royal Central The Palm's unnamed bar and pool bar.** Only OTA listings exist, with no venue names.
- **Radisson Beach Resort Palm's Chinawhite Sky Pool & Bar.** Only the French and German hotel pages are indexed, the hotel phone numbers conflict, and an OTA lists the hotel under "Dixcove, Ghana".
