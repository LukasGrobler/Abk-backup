> **Method and limits. Read before using any contact detail.** This session's outbound proxy refused every direct page fetch (WebFetch returned DNS errors; curl CONNECTs were rejected with 403 for every host, including example.com, Instagram, Facebook, Tripadvisor, Zomato and Time Out). The shared web-search budget ran out after about 40 searches. So **all facts below come from search-engine result summaries of the cited pages, not from opening the pages directly**. Two consequences follow. (1) I could not run any technical website checks: copyright year, `<meta viewport>`, SSL errors, parked domains. Website evidence is limited to what the search results showed (no site, hotel sub-page only, misspelled domain, HTTP-only URL, stale or conflicting listings). (2) I could not retrieve Instagram follower counts or last-post dates. **Phone numbers and emails must be re-verified before outreach.** No number or email was guessed or pattern-built; anything not seen in a source is marked "not found".

## Key Question 1: Which bars and clubs exist in Bur Dubai, Karama, Oud Metha and the old west-of-Creek areas today, and which are the best leads?

### Takeaway
The area's nightlife sits almost entirely inside old 2–4 star hotels. These are mainly along Khalid Bin Al Waleed Rd, Al Mankhool Rd, Kuwait St, Al Mina Rd, Al Fahidi/Meena Bazaar and Sheikh Khalifa Bin Zayed Rd (Karama). The mix is Indian/Bollywood dance bars, Filipino band clubs, Russian clubs, Nepali clubs, and a few old British and Irish pubs and sports bars. I identified **36 candidate venues** (31 with usable detail and 5 low-data names). Nearly all lack their own website and depend on aggregator directories such as Expat Nights, Nights in Dubai, Nightlife of Dubai, Dizzer and Zomato. Those directories hold stale or contradictory data. Operating status for October 2026 could not be confirmed directly for any venue, so most are marked "unverified".
