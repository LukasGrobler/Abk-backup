## Q1. Which bars, pubs, lounges and nightclubs exist in Deira, Garhoud, Festival City and the airport area today?

### Takeaway
Deira's nightlife is still concentrated in two places: old 3–4 star independent hotels around Al Rigga, Al Muteena, Baniyas Square, Al Maktoum Road and Abu Hail, and the bars of a handful of chain hotels (Radisson Blu Deira Creek, Crowne Plaza Deira, Hyatt Regency Corniche, Le Méridien and Millennium Airport in Garhoud, plus the Festival City IHG hotels). Many of the independent hotels' clubs are only described as an unnamed "nightclub" amenity on hotel-booking sites, and that is the strongest prospect signal in the area. I found 25 in-scope venues; most could not be confirmed open in 2026.
