# Central Dubai gap-fill (SZR / Trade Centre / Za'abeel / DIFC / Downtown / Business Bay / Al Habtoor City / Jumeirah 1 / La Mer / City Walk): additional under-marketed bars, lounges, pubs and clubs, with public business contacts

> **Method and coverage warning (read first).**
> - **Sources.** Everything here comes from WebSearch result summaries: **35 searches, the full allowance**. Page fetching (WebFetch and curl) was blocked, so no page was opened first-hand. Nothing here comes from a website audit (footer year, mobile layout, SSL), and no Instagram or Facebook profile was viewed.
> - **Status.** "Status" is a judgement from listing dates. No venue's October 2026 trading status could be confirmed first-hand, so most rows read **"unverified"** with a qualifier.
> - **Contacts.** Every phone number and email is copied from the cited result. None is guessed or built from a pattern, and "not found" means not found. Hotel switchboard and hotel-wide inboxes are labelled as such. Staff names and personal mobiles were left out on purpose.
> - **Instagram.** **No Instagram handle or follower count surfaced for any venue.** Profiles cannot be fetched, and searches for handles returned unrelated accounts.
> - **Scores.** The **opportunity score** (1–5, where 5 = weakest online presence and biggest opportunity) is the researcher's judgement from the cited evidence. It is not a sourced fact.
> - **No duplicates.** Venues already in `szr_downtown_difc_business_bay_jumeirah.md` are not repeated: Boston Bar, Brauhaus, Long's Bar, Harvesters, Double Decker, Blue Bar, Bar Sport/Canal Lounge, Fibber Magee's, Bar Allegra, Bridgewater Tavern, Soul Kitchen, Opal Room, Harry Ghatto's, Bar Dalí, Neos and Zuma.

## Which of the earlier pass's unverified candidates are open in 2026 and under-marketed, and which further central-Dubai bars/clubs have weak online presence?

### Takeaway
This pass adds **30 new venues** to the lead list:
- **14** of the earlier pass's ~25 unverified candidates now have names, hosts and at least a hotel switchboard.
- **16** further bars and lounges were found. They sit mostly in Business Bay hotels and in the **voco Dubai tower on SZR**, where four separately named lounges on floors 49–51 have messy, mobile-only contact data.

No venue's 2026 trading status could be confirmed first-hand.

The strongest new leads are:
- **voco Dubai's sky lounges** (Windows Bar & Lounge, O Lounge, Black Mirror, Bar 51 / Room 505)
- **Teatro** (Towers Rotana), the same buyer as Long's Bar
- **Trader Vic's** (Crowne Plaza SZR)
- the **unnamed bar at ibis World Trade Centre**
- **Lilly's Social House** (Canal Central Hotel)
- **Sho Cho** (Dubai Marine Beach Resort)
- **Stars 'N' Bars** (La Mer)
