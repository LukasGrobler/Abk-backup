> **Research-coverage warning (read first).** This file covers **11 in-scope venues with sourced data (10 open or likely open, plus 1 confirmed closed)**. The target was 35 or more. Three tool limits stopped the work early:
> 1. **WebFetch** failed on every domain tried (rotana.com, timeoutdubai.com, whatson.ae, bayut.com, sothebysrealty.ae, accidentaltravelwriter.com) with `getaddrinfo ENOTFOUND`.
> 2. **curl through the session proxy** was refused with `CONNECT tunnel failed, response 403` / `connect_rejected` for every host tried, including rotana.com, google.com, whatson.ae, bayut.com and curlytales.com. As a result, **no direct website audits were possible**: no checks of footer copyright year, `<meta name="viewport">`, SSL errors, redirects or parked domains.
> 3. The **shared WebSearch budget** (200 calls per turn across all agents) ran out after 12 searches from this researcher.
>
> All findings below therefore come from search-result content (directory listings, hotel pages, articles) as summarised by the search tool. They are not first-hand page audits. Contact details are copied only from those results and are never guessed. Many listings conflict, and each conflict is flagged. A list of unverified candidate venues for a follow-up pass is in the Gaps section of Key Question 1.

## Which bars/clubs exist in this central area today (long-running and newer)?

### Takeaway
The verified, long-running, under-marketed core of this area is the old SZR and Satwa hotel pubs:
- **Long's Bar** (Towers Rotana)
- **Harvesters** (Crowne Plaza / Millennium Plaza Downtown)
- **Double Decker** (now Swissôtel Al Murooj, formerly Al Murooj Rotana)
- **Blue Bar** (Novotel WTC)
- **The Boston Bar** and **Brauhaus** (Jumeira Rotana)

There is also the independent **Fibber Magee's** (SZR, behind Saeed Tower). Business Bay adds **Bridgewater Tavern** (JW Marriott Marquis) and two smaller hotel bars at recently rebranded or mid-market hotels: **Grand Mercure Business Bay**, which was the Gulf Court Hotel until early 2026, and **Barceló Business Bay**. **Harry Ghatto's** (Emirates Towers) is closed.

### Inferences
- The best leads in this area are **old hotel pubs inside mid-market or rebranded hotels**: Rotana, Swissôtel (ex-Rotana), Novotel, Crowne Plaza / Millennium and Grand Mercure (ex-Gulf Court). Outlet marketing there is usually reduced to a hotel sub-page and a coded F&B email. Hotel rebrands (Al Murooj Rotana → Swissôtel; Gulf Court → Grand Mercure) leave stale directory listings behind, which is a concrete and easy-to-show pain point for a sales pitch.
- Jumeira Rotana (Boston Bar plus Brauhaus) and Towers Rotana (Long's Bar) share a Rotana corporate web template. A single approach to Rotana area F&B management could open several outlets at once.
- Business Bay's newer openings (Bar Dalí, the ME / Opus venues, the Taj and the JW Marriott Marquis flagships) skew towards strong marketing. The under-marketed Business Bay leads are more likely in 3–4★ hotels (Grand Mercure, Barceló) and independent venues such as Soul Kitchen.

Footer-year, viewport and SSL evidence could not be gathered in this session.
