> **Research limitations (read first).**
> 1. **Search summaries only.** Page fetching was blocked (per the brief, no WebFetch or curl was attempted). Every fact below comes from WebSearch result summaries. Sometimes a summary drew on several pages without saying which one held a given fact. Where that happened, the row cites the group of result pages and says so.
> 2. **Budget used.** All 35 allowed searches were used. Grosvenor House, Jannah, Dusit, Ramada, Barceló, Radisson-type mid-range Marina hotels, Staybridge, Novotel/ibis and Al Sufouh inland hotels were not searched. Instagram handles and follower counts were not searched, except where a summary happened to show one.
> 3. **No 2026 confirmation.** For no venue could I confirm from a 2026 source that it is trading. Status is "unverified" unless stated, and the newest evidence date is given where known.
> 4. **Contacts.** Every phone or email below comes from a cited source. None is guessed. Almost all numbers are **hotel switchboards**, labelled as such.
> 5. **Overlap with earlier notes.** I checked against `marina_jbr_jlt_barsha_tecom.md`. None of the venues below is in that file's lead table. The one exception is **Observatory Bar & Grill**, which the earlier file named but never assessed. It is assessed here as row 11 and **not counted** as new.

## Which bars/clubs exist in these sub-areas in 2026, and which are under-marketed (no own website, hotel sub-page only, stale or conflicting listings, inactive socials)?

### Takeaway
**24 new venues** plus one re-assessed (Observatory) were found. Almost all are hotel outlets in Dubai Marina (Marina Byblos, Wyndham/First Collection, InterContinental, Crowne Plaza, Stella Di Mare, JW Marriott Marina, Millennium Place) and Media City (Media One, Radisson Blu, Grand Plaza Mövenpick).

The weak-presence pattern is consistent. No venue has its own website or a direct line. Venue names conflict across listings (Marina Byblos pub "Nell Gwynne" vs "Le Green"; Radisson's rooftop "SomePlace Else" vs the hotel's own "Tamanya Goes Thai" page). Bars sit inside hotels that have rebranded (Wyndham Dubai Marina → The First Collection Dubai Marina; Address Dubai Marina → JW Marriott Hotel Marina). Some bars appear only in booking-site amenity text, with no name at all.

Al Quoz produced no licensed bar or nightclub in results; the one lead found is a daytime café/vinyl space. Knowledge Park/Internet City produced only one hotel lounge bar plus one hotel whose alcohol licence is disputed. Al Sufouh inland produced nothing.
