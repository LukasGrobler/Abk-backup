## Key Question 1: Which bars and clubs exist in the uncovered suburbs in 2026, and which are under-marketed?

### Takeaway
The previously uncovered districts hold few licensed venues, and nearly all are inside hotels or golf clubs. The best new leads are:
- **Millennium Place Mirdif**, which press describes as the only licensed property in Mirdif. Its outlets are The Urban Bar, a rooftop pool bar and Sala de Cigar.
- **Qube Sports Bar** at The Track, Meydan Golf, which has four conflicting phone numbers and stale coverage.
- **71 Sports Bar / "Stoke House"** at Trump International Golf Club, DAMAC Hills, where three generations of outlet names are still in circulation.
- **The Bricks Sports Bar** (ibis Styles Dragon Mart) and **Mr Toad's Dragon Mart** (Premier Inn) in International City.
- The Dubai South airport hotels: **Aloft (W XYZ Bar)** and **Holiday Inn Al-Maktoum Airport**.
- The re-flagged **Holiday Inn JVC** (formerly Avalon).
- The new bars at **The Meydan Hotel** (Saddle Bar and Equus) since its Kerzner relaunch in January 2026.

No licensed venue surfaced in Al Warqa, Muhaisnah, Ras Al Khor, Arjan, JVT or the new suburbs next to Festival City.
